What it does

One workspace for signals that are usually reviewed apart

Authentication events, device fingerprints, network origin and payment behaviour rarely sit in the same tool. RiskFusion AI joins them into a single incident record and shows what that combination means.

Correlated timelines

Related events are assembled into one ordered narrative per incident, so the sequence of an attack reads end to end instead of across four consoles.

Explainable scoring

A weighted signal engine shows which factors moved a score and by how much. Analysts can defend a decision because they can see how it was reached.

Recorded response

Containment steps are ranked by impact, and every action an analyst takes is written to the incident's audit timeline under their name.

How it works

From raw telemetry to a decision you can justify

STEP 01

Ingest

Identity, device, network and payment events arrive against a customer or session and are held on a single incident record.

STEP 02

Correlate

Weighted rules test each signal — impossible travel, unfamiliar devices, behavioural deviation — and combine them into a scored assessment with a written rationale.

STEP 03

Respond

Analysts triage from the queue, work the evidence, and record containment actions that persist to the incident's history.
